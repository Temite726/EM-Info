Erythromelalgia:

Erythromelalgia (EM) is a chronic pain condition characterized by episodic, burning pain associated with erythema and warmth of the extremities (See Resource 1 from Mayo Clinic). The feet and hands are most commonly affected. There are two main kinds, Primary EM and Secondary EM. To provide a brief overview, primary EM is caused by a specific gene with symptoms usually appearing within the early stages of life (Mayo Clinic). Secondary EM is when the condition is caused by another underlying condition (Mayo Clinic). In Secondary EM, treating the underlying cause will, in most cases, resolve the erythromelalgia. A majority of patients have an unknown cause, unfortunately (Resources 4 provides a nice flow chart for this).
