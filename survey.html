Survey Overview:

The survey is a basic questionnaire for people with erythromelalgia to collect data on the condition and get a better idea of how the condition affects people. Any submissions will be publicly available and anonymous. The form just requires an email to help keep track of responses. Any and all information that can be provided is greatly appreciated and I deeply thank anyone who spends the time filling the questionnaire out!

There are four sections on the form, however, you do not need to fill everything out to record your responses. While the more you can add the better, the most important section is part one, so if you don't have time or don't feel comfortable with sharing anything else that's okay. The results will be recorded in a public spreadsheet and eventually displayed using graphics in the Survey Stats page.
