Erythromelalgia
Info Site

EM info is a web page and survey aimed to provide increased access to information regarding the rare health condition erythromelalgia. The website contains an introduction to erythromelalgia, my own story and advice living with it, a survey to collect data, and a viewing section for certain parts of the survey (currently in development). The survey is intended to be filled out by erythromelalgia patients, aiming to collect some quantitative and qualitative data on the condition and its potential treatments.
